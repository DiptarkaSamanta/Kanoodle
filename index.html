How To Play Kanoodle

Goal: Fit all colorful polysphere pieces into the sockets without any overlaps or gaps!
Locked Pieces: Starter pieces marked with 🔒 are fixed by the challenge card. Place the remaining pieces to solve!
Controls:
  • Click Piece Pick up piece from tray
  • R or Scroll Wheel Rotate piece 90°
  • F Flip / mirror piece
  • Left Click Board Place piece into sockets
  • Click Placed Piece Pick it back up
  • Esc or Right Click Deselect
  • Right Click + Drag Rotate 3D camera
Smart Tools: Use Hint 💡 for a single-piece nudge, or Auto Solve ⚡ to watch the exact-cover algorithm complete the puzzle!
